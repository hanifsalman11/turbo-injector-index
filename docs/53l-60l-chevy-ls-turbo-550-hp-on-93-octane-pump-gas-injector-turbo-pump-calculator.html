Frequently Asked Questions (5.3L / 6.0L Chevy LS Turbo (550 HP on 93 Octane Pump Gas))

What size fuel injectors do I need for 5.3L / 6.0L Chevy LS Turbo (550 HP on 93 Octane Pump Gas) (550 HP)?

Using the forced-induction BSFC formula ($\text{lb/hr} = [\text{HP} \times \text{BSFC}] \div [\text{Cylinders} \times 0.80\text{ IDC}]$), 550 HP × 0.55 BSFC ÷ (8 cylinders × 0.80) equals 47 lb/hr (490 cc/min) per injector at 43.5 PSI base fuel pressure.

How many lb/min and CFM of turbo airflow does 550 HP require?

A modern turbocharged engine produces approximately 10 Crank HP per 1.0 lb/min of mass airflow. Reaching 550 HP requires ~55.0 lb/min (780 CFM) at a compressor pressure ratio of 1.88:1 (11 PSI manifold boost).

What size LPH fuel pump do I need for 550 HP on 93 Octane Gas?

At 550 HP and 0.55 BSFC, your injectors consume ~180 LPH at wide-open throttle. Adding a 22% safety buffer at 54 PSI rail pressure requires 220 LPH (Single 255 LPH / 340 LPH In-Tank Pump (Walbro GSS342 / Aeromotive 340)).
